Browser support

The refraction uses an SVG filter as backdrop-filter, which only Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Arc) support. Safari and Firefox show frosted glass. When the user prefers reduced transparency, the glass is opaque.

Props

PropTypeDefaultDescriptionRequired
childrenReact.ReactNode-Content rendered on top of the glass.No
classNamestring-Extra classes for the glass surface. Set its size here.No
contentClassNamestring-When set, wraps children in a div with these classes.No
radiusnumber999Corner radius in px. The default gives a pill or a circle.No
bevelDepthnumber14Width of the curved rim that bends the light, in px.No

aberrationnumber4Spread of the color fringe at the rim, in px. 0 turns it off.No
frostnumber0.4Blur applied after the refraction, in px.No
tintnumber0.12Opacity of the white body tint, from 0 to 1.No
