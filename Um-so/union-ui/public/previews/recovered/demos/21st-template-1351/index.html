Asked at the counter

Before you ask.

Do I need to give you my passcode?

For screens, batteries, ports and cameras, no: we test those with the phone locked. For board and data work we ask for it on the ticket and delete it from our notes when the job is closed.

Will I lose my photos and messages?

A screen, battery or port repair never touches your storage. Back up anyway if you can; for water and board repairs we tell you the risk before we start.

What if you cannot fix it?

You pay nothing beyond the free diagnosis. If a part has to be ordered just for you, we say so before ordering it.

Do you fix water damage?

Yes. Bring it in switched off, and skip the bag of rice. We clean the board in an ultrasonic bath and tell you within a day what survived.

What does the warranty cover?

The part we fitted and our work on it, for twelve months from the ticket date. Drops and new water damage are not covered, but we will still look at it for free.

Do you buy broken devices?

We do, for parts or to rebuild for the refurbished shelf. Bring it in and we make an offer after the diagnosis.
