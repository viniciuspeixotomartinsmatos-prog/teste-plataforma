Getting started

Start with a result: a research question, a launch plan, a page of notes, or a brief that needs a first shape. The agent works out the steps.

A plain sentence is enough. Say what you want, what you already have, and any limit that matters. Add detail as the work takes shape.

Your instructions, notes, and source material are the context. Bring the background you would share with a collaborator.

Pick the model that fits the job. Faster ones for drafts and exploration. Stronger ones when the answer has to be careful.

Working with agents

Ask for a narrower focus, a different tone, or another pass. You review the result and decide what happens next.

Something you can read and use: a brief, a comparison, a plan, or a draft. The examples keep the source and the next step beside the result.

The connection scenes show how context from tools could sit next to the work. They are demonstrations. Those tools are not live connections on this page.

Say what is off-limits and what must stay true. The agent follows the brief you give it — you stay in charge of the call.
