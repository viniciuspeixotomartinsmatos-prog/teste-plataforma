Do my clients need an account to book?

No. Clients pick a time, add their details and get a confirmation by email. There is nothing to sign up for.

Which calendars does Hourline work with?

Hourline connects to the calendars most teams already use, and to any calendar that shares a standard feed. It checks every connected calendar before it offers a time.

How does round robin choose who gets a booking?

It looks at who is free at that time, who has had the fewest bookings this week, and any rules you add, such as language or region.

Can I take payment when someone books?

Yes, on Business. Ask for a deposit or the full price when the client books, and refund it in one click if you cancel.

What happens when a client needs to reschedule?

Every confirmation has a link to move or cancel the booking. The new time follows your rules, and the host hears about it straight away.

Can I try Team before I pay?

Yes. Team is free for 14 days with up to 10 seats, and you keep your pages if you move back to Solo.
